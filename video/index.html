How it works

  • Plays everywhere. iPhones often record in HEVC, which many Android phones can't play. The video is re-encoded as H.264 with AAC audio in an MP4, the format both platforms play natively.
  • Smaller, still sharp. Phone cameras record at very high bitrates. Bringing it down to a sensible bitrate for the resolution cuts the size a lot with little visible difference.
  • Starts playing straight away. The file's index is placed at the front, so it can start playing before it has fully downloaded.
  • Private. Your video never leaves your device. Conversion uses your phone or computer's built-in video encoder.
